Melbourne Youth Support Network is growing our team of youth workers, volunteer coordinators and community support staff. Explore our current opportunities below — each role lists the responsibilities, requirements and how to apply.

Community Support Worker

Reference number
CSW214
Salary
$68,000 – $74,000 per annum (pro rata for part-time)
Reports to
Community Programs Manager

Provide direct, practical support to young people and families accessing our community programs, helping them connect with local services and build long-term wellbeing.

Key responsibilities

  1. Complete intake interviews and assess each client's needs.
  2. Develop and review individual support plans with clients.
  3. Coordinate referrals to housing, health and education services.
  4. Maintain accurate, confidential case notes and records.
  5. Attend weekly team case-review meetings.

Essential requirements

  • Diploma of Community Services or a related qualification.
  • Current Working with Children Check and National Police Check.
  • At least one year of experience in a frontline support role.
  • Current Victorian driver's licence.

Preferable requirements

  • Experience supporting culturally and linguistically diverse communities.
  • Current First Aid and CPR certificate.
  • Knowledge of Melbourne's youth and family service system.

Volunteer Engagement Coordinator

Reference number
VEC091
Salary
$72,000 – $80,000 per annum
Reports to
Operations Manager

Recruit, train and support our volunteer network so that volunteers are well matched to programs and feel valued in their contribution to the community.

Key responsibilities

  1. Recruit and onboard new volunteers across all programs.
  2. Plan and deliver regular volunteer induction and training sessions.
  3. Build volunteer rosters for community events and outreach.
  4. Check in with volunteers and resolve issues as they arise.
  5. Organise volunteer recognition and appreciation events.

Essential requirements

  • Experience coordinating volunteers, teams or community groups.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.
  • Current Working with Children Check.
  • Confident using rostering or database software.

Preferable requirements

  • Qualification in community development, human resources or event management.
  • Experience with volunteer-management platforms such as Better Impact.
  • Availability for occasional evening and weekend events.

Youth Outreach Worker

Reference number
YOW338
Salary
$65,000 – $70,000 per annum (pro rata for part-time)
Reports to
Youth Services Team Leader

Work alongside young people aged 12–25 in their own communities, running outreach activities and mentoring that build confidence, connection and trust.

Key responsibilities

  1. Deliver after-school, school-holiday and drop-in youth programs.
  2. Build trusted relationships with young people and their families.
  3. Recognise early signs of disengagement and respond appropriately.
  4. Refer young people to specialist support services when needed.
  5. Record session attendance and program outcomes.

Essential requirements

  • Certificate IV in Youth Work or a related qualification.
  • Current Working with Children Check and National Police Check.
  • Availability for after-school, evening and some weekend shifts.
  • Current Victorian driver's licence.

Preferable requirements

  • Lived experience of, or a strong connection to, the local community.
  • Experience facilitating group activities or workshops.
  • Training in Youth Mental Health First Aid.

Working at MYSN

We are a not-for-profit, so our people stay because the work matters. In return we offer:

  • Flexible and hybrid working arrangements
  • Salary packaging that increases your take-home pay
  • An annual professional-development budget
  • Additional paid leave over the end-of-year shutdown
  • Regular clinical supervision and a free employee assistance program

How to apply

  1. Note the six-character reference number for the role you want.
  2. Open the Apply page and complete the form.
  3. Enter the reference number and respond to the listed requirements.
  4. Submit your application before the closing date shown above.

Melbourne Youth Support Network is an inclusive employer. We welcome applications from people of all backgrounds and abilities, and we strongly encourage Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people to apply.
